ES 1310 Intro to CAD

Reza Ebadi

Assistant Professor of Teaching

Reza Ebadi teaches introductory computer-aided design at Worcester Polytechnic Institute, helping students turn ideas into precise digital models.

Course Description

ES 1310 introduces students to computer-aided design as a tool for engineering communication and problem solving. Students learn to create 2D sketches, 3D parts, and assemblies, and to produce clear technical drawings from their models.

Through weekly hands-on projects, students build the habits of good modeling practice: planning a design, applying constraints, and documenting the result. The course prepares students for design work in later courses and projects.
